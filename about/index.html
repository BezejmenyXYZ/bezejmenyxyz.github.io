🔍 Full Transparency

📈 Financial Commitment:

Policy: Reinvest income until the server is self-sustaining

Reporting: Regular Comprehensive Financial Updates

Access: Open Development Process Visibility

We believe transparency isn't just a buzzword – it's a fundamental pillar of trust and community building. Every registration fee, every generous donation, every supporter contribution gets strategically used to make Bezejmeny stronger and more stable. Our current priority is simple: cover operating costs and reinvest into infrastructure, development, and community events until the project can reliably pay for itself. After that, we may gradually recover part of the founders' own investment and allow modest tips for the time we dedicate – with that process communicated openly to our community.
