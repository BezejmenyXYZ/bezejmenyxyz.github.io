📖 About Bezejmeny

Building the ultimate Minecraft community experience

Our Story

🏗️ Rationale:

Designed specifically for:

NoName Squad QTY Trans*Cord All adventurers who want to join us!

Vision: A shared, endlessly growing community

Born for the passionate hearts of thriving communities, Bezejmeny represents the perfect fusion of QTY, TransCord, and NoName Squad. While we proudly honor our roots in these exceptional communities, our doors remain wide open to adventurers from every corner of the Minecraft universe who share our vision of epic gameplay.

  • Built by passionate gamers, for passionate gamers - we understand what the community truly craves
  • Startup mindset with entrepreneurial fire driving every strategic decision
  • Complete investment transparency - your support flows directly into development excellence
  • Multi-community heritage creating a uniquely diverse and inclusive gaming environment

🚀 The Journey

📊 Development Timeline:

Investment: More than $100 Personal Funds, and counting

Status: Alpha Testing → Closed Beta (now!) → Open Beta → Public Launch

Mission: Sustainable Community-Driven Platform

From late-night Discord brainstorming sessions to a fully-fledged startup venture, our journey has been fueled by pure passion and relentless dedication. Every Czech crown invested flows directly from our founders' personal wallets – no external investors, no corporate agenda – just genuine, unwavering love for the Minecraft community and its limitless potential.

🛠️ Where Your Support Goes:

  • Server Infrastructure: Premium hosting, DDoS protection, and bulletproof uptime reliability
  • Custom Development: Unique plugins, groundbreaking features, and innovative gameplay mechanics
  • Security & Monitoring: Advanced anti-cheat systems and 24/7 performance monitoring
  • Community Programs: Epic events, exciting competitions, and rewarding achievement systems
  • Future expansion and continuous innovation breakthroughs

🔍 Full Transparency

📈 Financial Commitment:

Policy: 100% Reinvestment into Project Excellence

Reporting: Regular Comprehensive Financial Updates

Access: Open Development Process Visibility

We believe transparency isn't just a buzzword – it's a fundamental pillar of trust and community building. Every registration fee, every generous donation, every supporter contribution gets strategically reinvested directly into making Bezejmeny the absolute best it can be. First, we cover our operational costs; then we invest in development, fund exciting prizes and community events; and any remaining resources go toward fair compensation for our dedicated work – every contribution helps build something extraordinary for our community.
