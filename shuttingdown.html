We're sorry... we're shutting down

What led to this decision?

Over the past few months, primary development on the project came to a halt. As a result, the server fell far short of our original vision and promised features—leaving core mechanics broken or missing entirely, which ultimately dropped player activity to zero.

Continuing to cover hosting and server maintenance costs out-of-pocket without an active player base or community support is no longer sustainable for us.

We would like to thank everyone who supported project Bezejmeny along the way.
