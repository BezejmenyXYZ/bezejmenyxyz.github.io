1. Any willing server player who has the role of Mate (at the latest on the day the event ends) may participate in the event (hereinafter referred to as “participant”).
2. The event is limited in time and no changes to the time frame are expected.
3. The event may end earlier, or without announcing the winner, with an explanation provided.
4. “Legal” acquisition of items is defined as obtaining items in survival mode, WITHOUT using item cloning, trading with other players for real currency or other material goods, or obtaining them in any other unnatural way or using known exploits.
5. The goal of the participants is to independently and legally obtain as many “helper shards” as possible.
6. The aforementioned “helper shards” can be purchased from one of the villagers in the spawn area for a set price - 1 shard costs: 64x Green dye and 64x Brown Dye. Both of these items must also be obtained legally.
7. The participant with the most “helper shards” at the end of the event is declared the winner.
8. The winner of the event may receive the rank of “Helper” on the newly created server bezejmny.xyz. 
9. An alternative prize for the winner and runner-up is the ability to transfer their buildings or items to the newly created server after its official launch.
    The winner has the option to transfer an area of 4 chanks and 9 item slots to the new server and receive the helper reward, or 9 chanks and 18 item slots.
    The runner-up will receive the option to transfer 1 chank and 3 item slots or 4 chanks.
The event is organized by Bezejmeny.
